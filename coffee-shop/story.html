A little about us

Rooted here.
Open to everywhere.

A small neighborhood coffee house with a big soft spot for good ingredients, good work, and the people who make it all worthwhile.

It started with a window seat

A simple idea, brewed slowly.

Fieldwork began in 2018 with two friends, a second-hand espresso machine, and a shared belief that a coffee shop could feel like an extension of your own kitchen.

We found a little corner on Alder Street, filled it with plants, books and mismatched chairs, and started making coffee for whoever walked through the door. The neighborhood made it theirs. We never looked back.

Today, we're still independently owned, still learning, and still happiest when the room is full of familiar faces and first-time hellos.

What matters to us

Small choices. Better cups.

01 / Sourcing

Know where it comes from.

We buy from growers and importers who value long relationships, fair prices and careful farming. Every bag has a story worth knowing.

02 / Craft

Care is the secret ingredient.

We dial in every morning, bake in small batches, and never stop asking how we can make the next cup a little better.

03 / Community

Make room at the table.

Local artists on the walls, neighborhood makers on the shelves, and a seat for anyone who needs one. That's the whole point.

Still learning, always brewing

There's always room for one more.

Whether it's your first visit or your five-hundredth, we'd love to make you feel at home.
